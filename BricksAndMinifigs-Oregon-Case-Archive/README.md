# Bricks & Minifigs Oregon Consignment Case Archive  
*A Public Investigation into the Keizer, Oregon LEGO Collection Seizure*

This repository documents the events surrounding the seizure, sale, and disputed handling of a large LEGO Star Wars collection consigned to the Bricks & Minifigs (BAM) franchise store in Keizer, Oregon. It serves as a public archive for investigators, journalists, LEGO community members, and anyone seeking transparency into what happened.

The goal of this project is to preserve evidence, present a clear timeline, and highlight patterns that may extend beyond a single store. This is not a legal filing — it is a community‑driven investigative record built on documentation, receipts, footage, and firsthand accounts.

---

## 📌 Background

In 2023, an 83‑year‑old grandfather and his son consigned a massive LEGO Star Wars collection — more than **780 sets** and **1,200 minifigures**, many new‑in‑box — to the Bricks & Minifigs store in Keizer, Oregon. The collection represented **15 years of shared collecting**, intended as an investment for the grandchildren’s future education.

For roughly a year, the consignment relationship functioned normally. The store’s then‑owner, **Chrystal Law (now Chrystal Gorman)**, sold items, issued receipts, and provided monthly payouts.

That changed in **November 2024**, when Bricks & Minifigs corporate seized the Keizer store during a dispute with the franchise owner. During this takeover, the family’s consigned collection — which they still legally owned — was seized along with store inventory.

Evidence, including security footage, shows corporate leadership acknowledging the consignment agreement. Despite this, the new operator, **Brandon Best**, allegedly:

- Removed identifying labels from consigned items  
- Continued selling the collection  
- Denied knowledge of the consignment  
- Refused to allow inspection of the back room  
- Changed his story multiple times  
- Told the consignor to “take us to court”  

Attempts to resolve the matter through police, attorneys, and corporate channels were met with dismissal or silence.

This repository exists to document the case in full.

## 🎯 Purpose of This Archive

This repository aims to:

- Preserve evidence in a transparent, public format  
- Support investigative efforts into the incident  
- Provide journalists with a reliable source of documentation  
- Help other consignors or franchisees identify similar patterns  
- Ensure the story is not buried or forgotten  

This is a **community‑driven investigative project**, not a legal claim. All allegations are supported by available documentation and are presented for public awareness and research.
