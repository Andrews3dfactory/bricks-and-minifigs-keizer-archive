# Contributing Guidelines  
*How to safely and responsibly contribute to this public investigation*

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the Bricks & Minifigs Oregon Consignment Case Archive.  
This repository exists to preserve evidence, support investigative work, and provide transparency for the LEGO community and the public. Because this case involves real people, businesses, and potential legal implications, contributions must follow strict guidelines to ensure accuracy, safety, and integrity.

These rules protect contributors, protect the families involved, and maintain the credibility of the archive.

---

## 🧱 What You *Can* Contribute

You are welcome to contribute **public, verifiable, or well‑documented information**, including:

### ✔️ Public Records
- News articles  
- Court filings  
- Business registrations  
- Franchise documents  
- Publicly accessible police reports  

### ✔️ Public Social Media Content
- Screenshots of public posts (with URLs and timestamps)  
- Archived versions of deleted posts (e.g., Wayback Machine)  
- Public statements from Bricks & Minifigs corporate or franchise owners  

### ✔️ Evidence Documentation
- Photos of the Keizer store during or after the takeover  
- Screenshots of messages or emails (with personal info redacted)  
- Receipts, contracts, or inventory lists you have legal rights to share  

### ✔️ Legal or Analytical Contributions
- Relevant statutes or case law  
- Commentary on franchise law, consignment law, or consumer protection  
- Comparisons to similar incidents in other BAM locations  

### ✔️ Firsthand Accounts
If you are a:
- Former BAM employee  
- Former franchisee  
- Consignor  
- Customer with relevant experience  

You may submit a **summary** of your experience.  
Do not include private personal information.

---

## 🚫 What You *Cannot* Contribute

To protect the integrity of the archive and avoid legal risk, **do not submit**:

### ❌ Private Personal Information
- Home addresses  
- Phone numbers  
- Email addresses  
- Financial information  
- Signatures (unless redacted)  

### ❌ Unverified Claims
- Rumors  
- Hearsay  
- Anonymous accusations without evidence  

### ❌ Confidential or Proprietary Materials
- Internal corporate documents not publicly released  
- Leaked materials you do not have permission to share  

### ❌ Harassment or Targeted Attacks
This archive documents behavior — not people’s private lives.  
No doxxing, no threats, no harassment.

---

## 🧽 Redaction Requirements

Before uploading any file, **you must redact**:

- Personal contact information  
- Home addresses  
- Phone numbers  
- Email addresses  
- Bank or payment details  
- Signatures (unless necessary and permitted)  

Redaction should be clear and obvious (e.g., black bars or blurred text).

---

## 📁 File Organization

Place files in the correct folder:

- `docs/evidence/` — Receipts, contracts, screenshots, transcripts  
- `docs/legal/` — Cease‑and‑desist letters, attorney responses, statutes  
- `docs/media/` — News articles, social media posts, public statements  
- `docs/analysis/` — Commentary, legal analysis, franchise patterns  

Use clear filenames, such as:

- `2024-11-18-police-report-redacted.pdf`  
- `2023-consignment-contract-redacted.pdf`  
- `2024-12-01-store-audio-transcript.md`  

---

## 🧪 Accuracy and Verification

Every contribution must be:

- **Accurate**  
- **Verifiable**  
- **Sourced** (when applicable)  
- **Clearly labeled** if it is analysis or opinion  

If you are unsure whether something qualifies, open an Issue first.

---

## 🔧 How to Submit a Contribution

1. **Fork** the repository  
2. **Create a new branch** for your contribution  
3. **Add your files** to the appropriate folder  
4. **Redact sensitive information**  
5. **Open a Pull Request** with:
   - A clear description of what you added  
   - Sources for any public records  
   - Context for why the file is relevant  

A maintainer will review your submission.

---

## 🛡️ Legal Disclaimer

By contributing, you confirm that:

- You have the legal right to share the materials you upload  
- Your contribution does not violate privacy laws or confidentiality agreements  
- You understand this repository is public and permanent  
- You are not providing legal advice  

The maintainers reserve the right to reject or modify contributions that pose legal, ethical, or safety concerns.
