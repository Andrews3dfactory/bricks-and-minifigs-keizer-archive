# Media Archive  
*News coverage, public statements, social media posts, and community reactions related to the Bricks & Minifigs Keizer consignment case*

This folder contains all publicly available media connected to the investigation into the handling, seizure, and disputed sale of a large LEGO Star Wars collection at the Bricks & Minifigs (BAM) store in Keizer, Oregon.  
These materials help document how the story has been discussed, shared, and interpreted by the public, the LEGO community, and the franchise network.

Media files in this archive include news articles, social media posts, public statements, and community discussions that provide context, visibility, and corroboration.

---

## 📁 Folder Structure

### **/news-articles/**
Contains:
- Local news coverage  
- Online articles  
- Community journalism pieces  
- Public reporting on the Keizer store takeover  
- Articles referencing the consigned LEGO collection  

These files help establish the public timeline and show how the story reached the broader community.

---

### **/social-media/**
Contains:
- Facebook posts from the Keizer store  
- Instagram photos of the collection on display  
- TikTok or YouTube videos featuring the store or the collection  
- Public comments from customers and community members  
- Screenshots of posts made by Chrystal Law / Gorman, Brandon Best, or BAM corporate  

These materials document how the collection was promoted, how the store communicated with customers, and how the community reacted during and after the corporate takeover.

---

### **/public-statements/**
Contains:
- Statements from Bricks & Minifigs corporate  
- Posts or announcements from franchise owners  
- Public comments made by individuals involved in the case  
- Clarifications or denials issued by any party  

These files help track how the narrative changed over time and how different parties attempted to frame the situation.

---

### **/community-discussions/**
Contains:
- Reddit threads  
- LEGO fan forum discussions  
- AFOL community reactions  
- Public conversations about consignment risks and franchise behavior  

These discussions provide insight into how the LEGO community interpreted the events and how similar incidents may have surfaced elsewhere.

---

## 🧾 Media Standards

All media included in this folder must meet the following criteria:

### ✔️ Publicly Accessible  
Only content that was publicly posted or published may be included.  
Private messages or private group posts are **not** permitted unless the contributor has explicit permission to share them and redacts personal information.

### ✔️ Archived When Possible  
Because posts can be deleted, contributors should include:
- URLs  
- Timestamps  
- Archive links (e.g., Wayback Machine, archive.ph)  

### ✔️ Redacted Where Necessary  
Remove:
- Personal phone numbers  
- Home addresses  
- Email addresses  
- Sensitive personal identifiers  

### ✔️ Contextualized  
Each file should include a short description explaining:
- What it is  
- When it was posted  
- Why it is relevant  

---

## 🧱 Why Media Matters in This Case

Media coverage and public posts help establish:

- The visibility of the consigned collection  
- The store’s public promotion of the items  
- Community awareness of the collection before the takeover  
- How the narrative changed after corporate seized the store  
- Conflicting statements made by individuals involved  
- Patterns of behavior across multiple BAM locations  

Media also helps investigators and journalists verify claims and understand how the story evolved in real time.
