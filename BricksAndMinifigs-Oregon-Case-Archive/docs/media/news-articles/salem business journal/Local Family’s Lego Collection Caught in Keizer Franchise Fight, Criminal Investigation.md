## 🧱 Key Facts from the Article

### **The Collection**
- 780+ LEGO Star Wars sets  
- 1,200+ minifigures  
- Estimated value: **$150,000–$200,000**  
- Built over 15 years as a family investment  

### **The Consignment Agreement**
- Signed **November 22, 2023**  
- Between **Bryan Mansell** and **Chrystal Law‑Gorman** (Bricks & Minifigs Salem‑Keizer LLC)  
- Store received **exclusive right to sell**  
- **Ownership remained with Mansell until sale**  
- **35% store / 65% consignor split**  
- Monthly payouts due **on or before the 15th**  
- Store required to maintain **full‑value insurance**  
- Agreement binding on **successors**  
- Automatic termination **January 31, 2025**  
- Unsold items must be returned within **10 days**  

### **Corporate Seizure (Nov 14, 2024)**
- Corporate removed the Gormans from the store  
- Inventory was seized  
- Consigned items were not separated  
- Security footage allegedly captured corporate acknowledging the consignment  
- Labels on consigned sets were later removed  

### **Conflicting Accounts**
- Corporate claims consignment was unauthorized  
- Gormans claim corporate knew from the beginning  
- Corporate denies responsibility for missing items  
- New operators deny knowledge of the consignment  
- Evidence contradicts these denials  

### **Evidence Reviewed by Journalists**
