# Analysis Overview  
*Legal, operational, and behavioral analysis of the Bricks & Minifigs Keizer consignment case*

This folder contains all analytical documents related to the investigation into the handling, seizure, and disputed sale of a large LEGO Star Wars collection consigned to the Bricks & Minifigs (BAM) store in Keizer, Oregon.  
While the `docs/evidence/` and `docs/legal/` folders contain raw documentation, this folder provides **interpretation**, **context**, and **expert‑level breakdowns** of what the evidence shows.

The goal of this section is to help investigators, journalists, and community members understand the broader implications of the case — legally, operationally, and ethically.

---

## 📁 Folder Structure

### **/legal-analysis.md**
A detailed breakdown of:
- Applicable Oregon statutes  
- Potential civil and criminal violations  
- Consignment law  
- Conversion (civil theft)  
- Bailment and property rights  
- Franchise obligations  
- Corporate liability  

This file explains what the law says, how it applies, and where the documented actions may conflict with legal requirements.

---

### **/franchise-patterns.md**
An investigation into:
- Similar incidents at other Bricks & Minifigs locations  
- Corporate seizure behavior  
- Inventory handling practices  
- Franchisee complaints  
- Publicly reported disputes  

This file helps determine whether the Keizer incident is isolated or part of a broader pattern.

---

### **/corporate-behavior.md**
A structured analysis of:
- Statements made by Bricks & Minifigs corporate  
- Actions taken during the Keizer store seizure  
- Conflicts between statements and documented behavior  
- Communication failures  
- Potential motives and incentives  

This file examines how corporate decisions shaped the outcome of the case.

---

### **/witness-statements.md**
Summaries of:
- Firsthand accounts  
- Employee statements  
- Customer observations  
- Franchisee experiences  
- Relevant public testimony  

All statements are anonymized unless the contributor has explicitly agreed to be identified.

---

## 🧱 Purpose of the Analysis Section

The analysis section exists to:

- Connect evidence to legal frameworks  
- Identify inconsistencies in statements or behavior  
- Document patterns across multiple franchise locations  
- Provide investigators with structured insights  
- Help journalists understand the broader context  
- Protect future consignors and collectors  

This section does **not** make legal conclusions — it highlights areas where the evidence suggests potential violations or systemic issues.
