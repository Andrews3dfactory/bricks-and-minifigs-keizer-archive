# Legal Documentation Archive  
*Cease‑and‑desist letters, attorney correspondence, statutes, and legal context for the Bricks & Minifigs Keizer consignment case*

This folder contains all legal‑related materials connected to the investigation into the handling, seizure, and disputed sale of a large LEGO Star Wars collection consigned to the Bricks & Minifigs (BAM) store in Keizer, Oregon.  
These documents help clarify the legal rights of the consignor, the obligations of the store, and the actions taken by attorneys and law enforcement.

This archive is not legal advice — it is a public record of what occurred and how the involved parties responded.

## ⚖️ Legal Themes Documented in This Archive

### **1. Consignment Law**
The consigned LEGO collection remained the legal property of the consignor at all times.  
Evidence in this folder shows:
- Ownership was never transferred  
- The store acknowledged the consignment agreement  
- Payments were made monthly for over a year  

### **2. Conversion / Civil Theft**
The alleged removal of labels, refusal to return property, and continued sale of consigned items may constitute conversion under Oregon law.

### **3. Franchise Seizure Issues**
Corporate seizure of a franchise location raises questions about:
- Inventory handling  
- Responsibility for consigned goods  
- Communication failures between corporate and franchise operators  

### **4. Pattern of Behavior**
Other Oregon BAM locations, including Canby, have reported similar issues during corporate takeovers.  
This folder helps document those parallels.
