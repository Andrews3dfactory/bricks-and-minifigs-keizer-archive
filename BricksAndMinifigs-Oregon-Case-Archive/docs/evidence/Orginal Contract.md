# CONSIGNMENT AGREEMENT  
**Between:**  
**Consignor:** Bryan Mansell  
**Consignee:** Bricks and Minifigs Salem‑Keizer LLC  
**Franchise Owner:** Chrystal Law‑Gorman  
**Date of Execution:** November 22, 2023  
**Termination Date:** January 31, 2025 (automatic)

---

## I. Purpose  
This Agreement grants Bricks and Minifigs Salem‑Keizer LLC the **exclusive right to sell** the Consignor’s Star Wars LEGO collection on consignment.

---

## II. Description of Consigned Property  
The consigned property includes:  
- 780+ LEGO Star Wars sets  
- 1,200+ minifigures  
- All items listed in the inventory spreadsheet provided by the Consignee on December 13, 2023.

---

## III. Access and Inspection  
The Consignor may inspect:  
- Inventory  
- Sales records  
- Storage areas  
- Pricing  
at any reasonable time upon request.

---

## IV. Ownership  
**The consigned merchandise remains the sole property of the Consignor until the moment of sale.**  
No transfer of ownership occurs prior to sale.

---

## V. Insurance & Liability  
The Consignee must:  
- Maintain insurance covering **full replacement value** of the collection  
- Protect against **fire, flood, theft, vandalism**  
- Accept responsibility for **all shortages, losses, or damage** while the items are in their possession

---

## VI. Pricing  
The Consignee may price items at their discretion but must act in good faith to obtain fair market value.

---

## VII. Commission  
- **35%** of gross sale price retained by the store  
- **65%** paid to the Consignor

---

## VIII. Payment Schedule  
Payments must be made:  
- **Monthly**  
- **On or before the 15th** of each month  
- Accompanied by receipts and itemized sales records

---

## IX. Termination by Consignor  
The Consignor may terminate the agreement at any time with written notice.

---

## X. Assignment & Successors  
- The Agreement is **binding on successors**  
- **Neither party may assign** their interests without **prior written consent** of the other  
- Any transfer of obligations (including to a new operator) requires written approval from the Consignor

---

## XI. Automatic Termination  
This Agreement automatically terminates on **January 31, 2025**.  
Upon termination, the Consignee must:

- Return **all unsold inventory** within **10 days**  
- Provide a final accounting  
- Pay all outstanding proceeds

---

## XII. Entire Agreement  
This document constitutes the full agreement between the parties.
