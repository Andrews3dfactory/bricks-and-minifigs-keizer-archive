# Evidence Archive  
*Documentation, receipts, transcripts, and visual records related to the Bricks & Minifigs Keizer consignment case*

This folder contains all evidence relevant to the investigation into the handling, seizure, and disputed sale of a large LEGO Star Wars collection consigned to the Bricks & Minifigs (BAM) store in Keizer, Oregon.  
The materials here form the factual backbone of the case and support the timeline, analysis, and public reporting found throughout this repository.

Evidence is organized into subfolders for clarity and ease of reference.

---

## 📁 Folder Structure

### **/receipts/**
Contains:
- Monthly payout receipts  
- Itemized sales lists  
- Transaction logs  
- Payment confirmations  

These documents verify that the consignment agreement was active, ongoing, and acknowledged by the store prior to the corporate seizure.

---

### **/contracts/**
Contains:
- The original consignment contract  
- Any amendments or addendums  
- Signed agreements between the consignor and store owner (Chrystal Law / Gorman)  

These documents establish legal ownership and the terms under which the collection was entrusted to the store.

---

### **/inventory-lists/**
Contains:
- Master inventory of all 780+ LEGO sets  
- Minifigure lists  
- Condition notes (sealed, used, complete, missing parts)  
- Valuation estimates  

These lists are essential for determining what was consigned, what was sold, and what remains unaccounted for.

---

### **/screenshots/**
Contains:
- Screenshots of messages, emails, and social media posts  
- Photos of the collection on display in the store  
- Images showing price tags, labels, and shelving arrangements  

These files help establish the timeline and confirm that the collection was publicly displayed and actively sold.

---

### **/video-transcripts/**
Contains:
- Transcripts of security footage  
- Audio recordings from in‑store conversations  
- Notable quotes from corporate representatives and store operators  

These transcripts include the critical moment when Bricks & Minifigs corporate acknowledged the consignment agreement during the store takeover.

---

### **/timeline-images/**
Contains:
- Photos and graphics used to illustrate the timeline  
- Visual documentation of store conditions before and after the takeover  
- Images showing missing labels, reorganized shelves, or removed inventory  

These visuals support the chronological narrative found in `TIMELINE.md`.

---

## 🧾 Evidence Standards

All evidence in this folder must meet the following criteria:

### ✔️ Verifiable  
Every file must be traceable to a real source — a receipt, a contract, a video, a public post, or a firsthand account supported by documentation.

### ✔️ Redacted Where Necessary  
Personal information such as:
- Home addresses  
- Phone numbers  
- Email addresses  
- Bank details  
must be removed before uploading.

### ✔️ Unaltered  
Except for redaction, evidence must not be edited, cropped, or modified in a way that changes meaning or context.

---

## 🧱 Why This Evidence Matters

This case involves:
- A family’s 15‑year LEGO legacy  
- A corporate store seizure  
- Conflicting statements from franchise operators  
- Potential mishandling of consigned property  
- A pattern of similar incidents in other Oregon BAM locations  

The evidence in this folder ensures that the story is documented transparently and accurately, allowing investigators, journalists, and community members to understand what happened and why it matters.
